About us

Built by people who believe connection is progress.

Founded in 2018, Converge is an independent software company with a simple conviction: the products people use every day should make the world feel smaller, not noisier.

We're a distributed team of engineers, designers and researchers working across 14 countries. We build in the open, ship carefully and measure success by what our customers are able to create.
